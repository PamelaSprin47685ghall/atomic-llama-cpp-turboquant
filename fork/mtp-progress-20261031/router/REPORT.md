## Design

`GGML_TENSOR_FLAG_NO_REUSE` retains a non-view allocation for the graph and prevents in-place reuse, including through its views. The allocator also tracks the flag in saved node/source allocation plans to prevent reusing a plan made with a different lifetime contract. The process-wide `GGML_TP5_MOE_ROUTER_SCRATCH` is unset/0 by default and must be exactly 1 to enable; in Qwen4Exp only, 512-expert/top-10 graphs with more than one token retain the direct F32 router projection. N1 and OFF do not set the flag. An unsupported graph shape fails closed. No new output marking or fake dependency is used. No selected tree, GPU/HIP code, RSZ_RT, or nrows==1 exception is changed.
